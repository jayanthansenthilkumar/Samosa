Master the Fold

Perfect Samosas, Step by Step

Follow this standard technique to shape tight, leak-proof triangular samosas with smooth sealed edges.

01

Thaw the Sheets

Remove required pack from refrigerator. Let sheets rest at room temperature for 10-15 minutes to gain optimal flexibility.

02

Prepare the Filling

Cook your potato, paneer, or minced meat filling. Ensure the filling has completely cooled down to room temperature before wrapping.

03

Form a Cone

Gently peel a single sheet. Fold one corner diagonally over to form a neat triangular pocket or cone shape.

04

Fill Generously

Add 1.5 to 2 tablespoons of cooked filling into the open cone. Leave 1/2 inch edge space at the top for sealing.

05

Seal with Flour Paste

Brush the open flap edges with a thick paste made of all-purpose flour and water. Press the flap firmly to seal tight.

06

Fry to Golden Crunch

Deep-fry in medium-hot oil (160°C - 170°C) for 3-4 minutes until golden, or spray with oil and air-fry at 180°C for 10-12 minutes.
